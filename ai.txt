# Christmas - PropFirmDiscount.com

Christmas prop firm deals and the discount codes of the firms that ran them.

## Dataset

58 deals from 28 prop firms, newest 2026-01-05.
Each row states the deal, its dates, the firm's discount code, and where to redeem it.
